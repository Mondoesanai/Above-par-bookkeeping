Our story

We handle the numbers.
You reap the rewards.

We’re passionate about helping businesses thrive — that’s the whole reason Above Par exists. Too many owners only see their real numbers once a year, in a rush before taxes. We built the opposite: a steady monthly rhythm where every account is reconciled, every report is current, and nothing is a surprise.

The payoff is quiet but real — faster answers from your CPA, cleaner financing conversations, and the confidence to make the next move without second‑guessing the data behind it. We work in QuickBooks or Xero, connect with read‑only access wherever we can, and hand your tax preparer a clean, CPA‑ready package at year‑end.

What “Above Par” means — three promises on every set of books

Precision

Reconciled to the penny, categorized consistently, and tied out to source documents. If the numbers don’t agree, we find out why before we close.

Clarity

Reports you can read without a finance degree, and a plain‑English summary each month of what happened and what to watch.

Partnership

Personalized service, not a ticket queue — a direct line to the person who actually knows your books.
