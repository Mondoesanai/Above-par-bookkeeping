Privacy note

How we handle your information

Last updated 2026. This is a plain‑language summary, not a legal contract. If you have questions, email debbie.aboveparbookkeeping@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form we receive the name, email address, phone number, and message you provide. When you book through the scheduling tool, that provider (Calendly) collects the details you enter to create the appointment. We don’t run advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, schedule and prepare for your consultation, and provide bookkeeping services if you become a client. We don’t sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us operate — for example our scheduling tool, email provider, and, for clients, the accounting and payroll platforms used to do the work. Each only receives what they need for that purpose. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept while we’re in contact and for a reasonable period afterward. Client records are retained as long as needed to provide services and to meet legal and professional record‑keeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete enquiry information we’re not required to keep. Email the address above and we’ll take care of it.

Security

We use reputable providers and restrict access to your information to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share.
